Jammu to Katra is 50 km of highway — about 1.5 hours — and it's the first leg of nearly every Vaishno Devi yatra. Fares look simple, but airport stands, night arrivals and season surge make the range wide. Here's the honest 2026 picture.

## Jammu to Katra taxi fares (2026)

| Vehicle | Seats | One-way fare | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sedan (Dzire/Etios) | 4 | ₹2,500–3,000 | Couples, small families |
| Ertiga / Rumion | 6 | ₹3,500–4,500 | Family + luggage |
| Innova / Crysta | 6–7 | ₹4,500–5,500 | Comfort, seniors |
| Tempo Traveller 12–17 seat | 12–17 | ₹5,000–7,500 | Groups, yatra parties |

- Night pickup (10 PM–6 AM): +₹200–500 driver allowance on most bookings
- Luggage-heavy arrivals (tempo-top boxes) may add ₹200–300
- Return-trip bookings usually discount 10–15% off two one-ways

## Airport vs railway station pickup

- **Jammu Airport (IXJ):** prepaid stand outside arrivals — convenient but quoted highest. Pre-booked cabs wait with a name board inside
- **Jammu Tawi Station:** prepaid booth at the exit; touts quote "Katra ₹1,500" then add charges per bag and per stop — agree the full fare *before* sitting in
- **Katra Railway Station:** if your train runs to Katra itself, you don't need this guide — the station is 2 km from Banganga

## Booking options compared

1. **Prepaid stand** — no haggling, but peak-season rates and no driver vetting
2. **Hotel arrangement** — convenient, typically 10–15% commission built in
3. **Direct operator booking (call/WhatsApp)** — fixed fare, named driver, flight/train tracking; usually the best rate for airport pickups
4. **App cabs (Uber/Ola)** — unreliable for outstation Katra drops; availability is hit-or-miss at Jammu airport

## The Katra drop: what to know

- Katra town closes to heavy vehicles at certain hours — expect the last 2 km on a smaller road; ask your driver where exactly the drop point is relative to Banganga
- Your hotel's gate matters: "Katra hotel" can mean a 5-minute or 25-minute walk to the trek start. Tell the operator your hotel name when booking — pickup and drop adjust to it
- Continuing to Srinagar or Patnitop after the yatra? Book it as one round-trip with the same operator — sequential one-ways cost 20–30% more
