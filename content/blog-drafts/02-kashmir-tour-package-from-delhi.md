Kashmir is the one-hour flight that feels like a different country — Dal Lake shikaras at dawn, Gulmarg's gondola above the clouds, Pahalgam's pine forests along the Lidder. Delhi travellers do this trip year-round, and the 5 nights / 6 days Srinagar–Gulmarg–Pahalgam circuit is the format that works. Here's the full plan with honest 2026 costs.

## Why 5 nights / 6 days is the right length

Fewer days and you rush Gulmarg or skip Pahalgam's valleys; more days usually just add a second Srinagar night. The classic split:

- **Day 1–2:** Srinagar (arrival, Mughal Gardens, Dal Lake)
- **Day 3:** Gulmarg (gondola, meadows)
- **Day 4–5:** Pahalgam (Betaab, Aru, Chandanwari valleys)
- **Day 6:** Srinagar houseboat night or departure

## Getting there from Delhi

| Option | Time | Cost (one way) | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flight DEL → SXR | 1h 25m | ₹3,500–9,000 | Best — book 6–8 weeks ahead for summer |
| Train to Jammu + taxi | 12–14 hrs | ₹700–2,500 + ₹3,000 | Budget-friendly, tiring |
| Direct train to Srinagar (via Katra) | ~13 hrs | ₹800–2,500 | New rail link — scenic through the Pir Panjal |

Flights: IndiGo, Air India and SpiceJet fly the route multiple times daily. June–August is peak — book early or fares double.

## Day-wise itinerary

### Day 1 — Arrive Srinagar

Land at Sheikh ul-Alam Airport, transfer to your hotel or houseboat (20–40 min). Evening shikara ride on Dal Lake — sunset slots sell out, book with your package. Visit the floating vegetable market if you're up before dawn.

### Day 2 — Srinagar sights

Mughal Gardens circuit (Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh, Chashme Shahi), Shankaracharya Temple for the valley panorama, and handicraft shopping — pashmina, papier-mâché, saffron. Buy saffron only with the Pampore seal.

### Day 3 — Gulmarg day trip (or overnight)

Drive 50 km (1.5–2 hrs) through the Pir Panjal foothills. The Gulmarg Gondola is the reason you come — Phase 1 to Kongdoori, Phase 2 to Apharwat at nearly 4,000 m.

- Ride the gondola **early** — Phase 2 closes often for wind/weather, and afternoon queues are brutal
- Winter (Dec–Mar): this is India's skiing centre — gear and instructors book out weeks ahead
- Summer: the meadow blooms, pony trails, and the world's highest golf course

### Day 4 — Srinagar → Pahalgam

90 km along the Lidder River (2.5–3 hrs). Afternoon: Betaab Valley and the riverside. Trout at a riverside restaurant — the cold water makes it the best fish in the Himalayas.

### Day 5 — Aru and Chandanwari

Local cab to Aru Valley (meadows, starter treks) and Chandanwari (the Amarnath route's base, glacier views). Pony rides available. Evening back to Srinagar — or take the houseboat night here.

### Day 6 — Departure

Last-minute saffron shopping, airport transfer. Keep 3 hours buffer — Srinagar airport security is famously thorough.

## Best time to visit Kashmir from Delhi

- **March–May:** Tulip bloom (Asia's largest tulip garden), 10–22°C — the most beautiful window
- **June–August:** Peak summer escape, everything open, biggest crowds and prices
- **September–November:** Chinar gold, crisp air, thin crowds — the underrated choice
- **December–February:** Snow season — Gulmarg skiing, frozen Dal, houseboat fireplaces

## Mistakes that waste a day

- Treating Gulmarg as a half-day — Phase 2 queues eat hours; start at dawn
- Not budgeting for local sightseeing cabs in Pahalgam (union cabs are separate from your tour cab)
- Booking flights before hotels in peak season — fares spike while rooms vanish
- Skipping the houseboat night — it's the most Kashmir thing you'll do
