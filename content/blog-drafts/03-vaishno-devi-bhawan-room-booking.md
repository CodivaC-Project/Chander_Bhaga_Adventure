Eight million pilgrims a year pass through Katra, and accommodation is the part that decides whether your yatra feels smooth or exhausting. You have two very different options: the Shrine Board's own guest houses, and Katra's private hotels. This guide covers both — prices, booking rules, and which area to pick.

## Option 1 — Shrine Board accommodation

The Vaishno Devi Shrine Board (SMVDSB) runs its own properties, from ₹150 dormitory beds to ₹1,500-family rooms. Clean, safe, and honestly run.

| Property | Where | Price range (2026) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Niharika Bhawan | Katra (Yatra counter area) | ₹800–1,500 |
| Shakti Bhawan | Katra | ₹600–1,200 |
| Vaishnavi Dham / Saraswati Dham | Near railway station | ₹400–1,000 |
| Dormitories (Banganga / Manokamna Bhawan) | Katra | ₹150–300 per bed |
| Bhawan-area cottages (Manokamna) | At the shrine | ₹800–1,500 |

### How to book

- **Official site:** maavaishnodevi.org → "Online Services" → Accommodation. Create an account, book against your yatra parchi
- **Booking window:** 60 days ahead — Navratri and summer weekend slots vanish within minutes of opening
- **Payment:** online; cancellation follows Shrine Board rules, mostly refundable before check-in
- **At the counter:** same-day rooms often available at the Katra enquiry office, but never count on it in season

## Option 2 — Private hotels in Katra

Katra has 200+ private hotels in three clusters, most within 10–20 minutes' walk of Banganga:

- **Near Banganga / Yatra parchi counter:** best for an early trek start; mid-range family hotels ₹1,500–3,500
- **Main Katra road (bus stand side):** bigger hotels with parking and restaurants; ₹2,000–5,000
- **Premium (KC Residency, Lemon Tree, Fortune Inn Riviera tier):** ₹3,500–8,000, most with shuttle to Banganga

**What to check before booking a private hotel:**

- Distance to Banganga in *walking minutes*, not "near Katra" claims
- Early check-in — most yatra trains reach Jammu at 5 AM and you'll want a room by 8
- Packed-breakfast option for pre-dawn trek starts
- Luggage storage for the trek day (you leave bags and climb light)

## Bhawan-area stays — should you sleep at the top?

If you're doing the yatra over two days or travelling with seniors, staying near the Bhawan beats descending at night:

- Shrine Board cottages at Manokamna Bhawan — the only official option at the top, book early
- Private rooms in the Bhawan complex are limited and basic
- Alternative: helicopter pilgrims stay at Sanjichhat (Book via Shrine Board)

## Which area is right for you?

| Traveller | Best pick |
| --- | --- |
| First-timer, one-day yatra | Katra hotel near Banganga |
| Family with kids | Main-road hotel, 2 nights, helicopter optional |
| Seniors | Shrine Board Niharika + helicopter to Sanjichhat |
| Budget solo | Shrine Board dormitory ₹150–300 |
| Navratri pilgrim | Whatever books 45+ days out — the town sells out |

## Costs at a glance (per night, 2026)

- Budget private hotel: ₹800–1,500
- Mid-range family hotel: ₹1,500–3,500
