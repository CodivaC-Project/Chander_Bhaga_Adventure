# Best Time to Visit Kashmir: Month-by-Month Guide (2026)

**Excerpt:** Kashmir month by month — tulips in April, skiing in January, saffron in October. Snow seasons, crowd levels, prices, and the one window most travellers never consider.

There's no wrong month for Kashmir — only the wrong month for *you*. A honeymoon in January and a trek in January are two different trips. Here's every month, honestly.

## Quick answer

- **Snow and skiing:** December–February (best snow: January)
- **Tulips and blossoms:** Late March–April
- **Green meadows, all passes open:** May–August
- **Autumn colours, best prices:** September–November

## Month by month

### March — spring arrives

10–18°C. Almond and cherry blossoms, gardens waking up. Crowds still thin. Occasional late snow at Gulmarg — you can ski and see blossoms in one week.

### April — tulip month

The Indira Gandhi Memorial Tulip Garden (Asia's largest) hits full bloom for ~3 weeks, usually early-to-mid April. 15–22°C. This is the most photographed window of the Kashmir year — book hotels 3–4 weeks out.

### May — perfect weather

18–28°C in Srinagar, snow still on the Apharwat peaks. Every valley accessible. Families start arriving — prices climb from mid-May.

### June — peak season begins

Summer holidays. Gulmarg and Pahalgam at their greenest. Book flights 6–8 weeks out; weekend Srinagar hotels sell out. Amarnath yatra (late June–August) brings pilgrim traffic on the Pahalgam and Baltal routes.

### July — green and busy

Monsoon reaches the valley but Kashmir's rain is gentler than the plains'. Meadow trekking at its best — the Great Lakes trek window opens. 20–30°C.

### August — saffron fields prep, meadows peak

High season continues. Saffron harvest comes at month's end in Pampore. Great Lakes and Tarsar Marsar treks run full.

### September — the insider's month

Our honest favourite: chinar trees begin turning gold, crowds drop after the first week, weather stays 15–25°C, and prices fall 15–20%. Apple harvest — roadside orchard stalls everywhere.

### October — golden autumn

The valley turns copper and gold. Crisp mornings (8–20°C), clear skies, the best photography light of the year. First snowfall usually dusts Gulmarg by late October.

### November — quiet and cold

5–15°C, bare trees, fewest tourists of the year. Hotels at their cheapest. Shikara rides in fog are hauntingly beautiful. Some high passes begin closing.

### December — snow season opens

Gulmarg's ski season starts; Dal Lake freezes at the edges. Christmas–New Year is a packed premium window — book a month out. Carry real winter layers; Srinagar nights hit −2°C.

### January — deep snow

The skiing month. Gulmarg's powder at its best, gondola running (weather permitting). Srinagar gets its classic snowfall — houseboats with kangri heaters and fireplaces. Roads can close temporarily; keep a buffer day.

### February — snow with thinner crowds

Skiing continues, queues shorter than December–January. Slightly warmer afternoons. Best snow-month value.

## Matching the season to your trip

- **Honeymoon:** April, September, or December (snow romance)
- **Family with kids:** May–June or September — safe weather, everything open
- **Trekking:** July–September (Great Lakes, Tarsar Marsar, Kolahoi)
- **Skiing:** Late December–February
- **Photography:** April (tulips) or October (chinar gold)
- **Budget travel:** September and November — low prices, great weather
